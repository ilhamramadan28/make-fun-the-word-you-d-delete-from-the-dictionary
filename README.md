One word to erase from the dictionary forever: **moist**.

The one word everyone agrees sounds wrong — deleting it leaves the language with no word that makes a whole room wince just by being said out loud.

`main.py` reads `entry.txt`, validates the gate (UTF-8, 1–77 characters, exactly one word) and writes `/output/entry.txt` with mode 0644.
